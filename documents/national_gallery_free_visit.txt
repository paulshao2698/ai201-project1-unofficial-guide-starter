Title: National Gallery of Art Free Admission
URL: https://www.nga.gov/visit
Category: free museums
Region: Washington DC
Retrieved: 2026-06-07

The National Gallery of Art plan-your-visit page states that admission is always free. Visitors do not need passes or tickets to enjoy the galleries, exhibitions, or guided tours, although some events require free registration.

The National Gallery of Art is open daily from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The Sculpture Garden has extended evening hours on selected summer days. This makes the National Gallery a reliable free or low-cost DC activity for students, especially for indoor plans during hot, rainy, or cold weather.

This source is useful for answering free DC activity questions because it explicitly supports the price claim. If recommending a specific event or tour, check whether registration is required.
