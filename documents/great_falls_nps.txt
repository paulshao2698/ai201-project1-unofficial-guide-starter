Title: National Park Service Great Falls Park Basic Information
URL: https://www.nps.gov/grfa/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm
Category: hiking
Region: Northern Virginia

Great Falls Park is a National Park Service site in Northern Virginia. It is known for waterfall views, hiking trails, overlooks, and rocky terrain.

The park can become crowded on weekends and holidays. Parking may fill during busy times, so visitors should plan ahead and arrive early.

This source is useful for comparing Great Falls with Burke Lake for hiking, trail running, scenery, parking, and weekend crowds.
