Title: Smithsonian Museums Free Admission
URL: https://www.si.edu/visit/museums
Category: free museums
Region: Washington DC
Retrieved: 2026-06-07

The Smithsonian museum visit page states that admission is free at all Smithsonian museums except Cooper Hewitt in New York City. Most Smithsonian museums are open every day of the year except December 25.

For a student asking for free things to do in DC, Smithsonian museums are a strong recommendation because they are free, indoor, and located across the National Mall, near the National Mall, elsewhere in DC, and Virginia. They are also useful during heat waves or bad weather because museums are indoor activities.

This source supports general free DC recommendations, but it does not by itself provide a complete current weekend event schedule. If the question asks "this weekend," pair this source with current event calendar sources.
